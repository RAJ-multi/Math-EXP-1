🏥 Patient Healthcare Services

👨‍⚕️

General Outpatient (OPD)

Comprehensive consultation for acute & chronic illnesses by qualified Medical Officers.

👶

Maternal & Child Health

Antenatal checkups (ANC), institutional delivery, post-natal care, and immunizations.

🧪

Diagnostic Laboratory

Free blood, urine, hemoglobin, malaria, sputum, and blood sugar testing under N-KHY scheme.

💊

Free Pharmacy

100% free essential medicines provided as prescribed by PHC doctors under Jan Aushadhi guidelines.

🚑

24/7 Casualty & Emergency

First-aid, emergency stabilization, snake-bite treatment, and referral transportation support.

💉

National Health Programmes

Free screening & care for TB (NTEP), Leprosy, Dengue, Malaria, and NCDs (Diabetes & BP).

📜 Citizen Charter & Patient Guidelines

What Patients Should Know Before Visiting PHC Thungani:

  • Registration: Please register at the counter with your **Aadhaar Card** or **ABHA ID** to generate your OPD card.
  • Free Services: All consultations, diagnostic lab tests, essential medicines, and maternal care are provided 100% free of charge.
  • Emergency Care: Emergency cases are given immediate priority over routine OPD lines.
  • Referrals: Cases requiring higher secondary/tertiary surgical or specialist care will be referred with official referral slips to the District Hospital.
